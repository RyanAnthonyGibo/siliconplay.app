Screenshots, screen recording, and a bug report I can act on

By Ryan Gibo · Updated 12 November 2026

A useful bug report is a small set of facts and a picture of the actual failure. “It crashed” is not a report I can act on. This page is what version 1.0.0 can capture, what I want in the mail, and what I will throw away. The address is siliconplay.app@gmail.com. It is the only contact address. I read it.

I build SiliconPlay in Brazil, under 28.148.946 RYAN ANTHONY GIBO - ME. The app is for Apple silicon, M1 through M6, including Pro, Max, and Ultra. It is not compatible with Intel Macs. I have tested it on macOS Tahoe and macOS 27 Golden Gate. A report from another macOS version is welcome. Name the version. Do not apologize for it.

What to include

  • The chip, including Pro, Max, or Ultra if that is the machine.
  • The macOS version, copied from About This Mac, not from memory.
  • SiliconPlay’s version. This guide is about 1.0.0. If the about box says something else, send what it says.
  • Whether the window was the Play tablet or the Google APIs tablet. “The rooted one” is the APIs image. “The one with the store” is the Play image.
  • The moment it failed: first-run download, JDK message, install, the picture, the keyboard, or Play sign-in.
  • The package shape if an install failed: one APK, a split set, or an XAPK. Those last two are not installed by this version, and knowing which you had saves a day.
  • The game or the package name. “A game” is not a package name.

What not to send

  • Your Google Play password, a game password, or a one-time code. I will not ask, and I do not want them in the mailbox.
  • A license key, unless I asked for that specific key in a reply. The app’s license path is described in the privacy notice. A bug report is not that form.
  • A screen recording of your whole desktop with other people’s messages on it. Crop to the guest and the error.
  • A theory that replaces the facts. Send the sentence on screen. You can add the theory after.

Screenshot and recording

Version 1.0.0 can save a screenshot and can record the screen. Both actions are named in the Shortcuts list inside the app. The key next to each label is drawn by the menu. It is not stored as a separate string in the build I am writing from, so I will not guess the chord and have you hit the wrong thing. Open Shortcuts and limits in the app and read it on your copy. If you cannot reach the menu because the guest is fullscreen, Escape leaves fullscreen first, then read the menu.

A still of the error is enough for a wrong dialog. A short recording is better when the failure is a flicker, a stuck finger, or a download that stops at a line of text. Ten seconds of the failure beat five minutes of the launcher.

If the failure is in the guest and the Mac app is only the window, say that. If the Mac app itself quit, say that, and send the time it quit. I do not have your crash log until you attach what macOS showed you.

Match the report to the failure

The download

Tell me whether the SDK folder was one you created or one you already used for Android Studio, and the exact sentence if the app stopped on the JDK stub. The expected JDK hint is brew install openjdk@17, then run the package step again. If your screen says something else, quote that instead of this page. The folder has to contain emulator, adb, and sdkmanager if you pointed at an existing SDK.

Install

Say whether you used Cmd+I, drag and drop, or adb. Say whether the instance was running. A stopped instance cannot take an install. If adb was involved, say what adb devices printed, with the adb from the SDK folder SiliconPlay is using. Do not paste a different computer’s adb output.

Picture

Say whether the Android home screen was visible. A bad game on a healthy home screen is a different bug from a guest that never drew. I have one published measurement, on a MacBook Pro with M3 Max, and I do not need you to chase it. I need the game’s own graphics settings and a picture. The performance page explains what I will and will not claim: game performance.

Input

Say whether you were in Typing, game keys, or direct touch, and whether you had accepted a keymap. “Keys do nothing” after you clicked Terminal is focus. Click the Android image and try once more before you record. If a finger stays down, Shift-Escape releases every virtual finger, then tell me if it happened again.

Play

Do not send the password. Send the sentence Play showed, and say which image you were on. The rooted APIs image has no store. That is not a sign-in bug.

Privacy of the report itself

The mail you send is the mail you send. The app’s anonymous usage counter is TelemetryDeck. It does not include a personal profile and it does not include advertising identifiers, and it is not a copy of your email. The website’s ads, when they are shown, are Google AdSense on siliconplay.app. They are not inside the guest, and they are not attached to your bug report. The privacy notice is the full account. A bug mail is you choosing to write.

If the request is access, correction, or deletion of personal data, say that in the first line so I do not file it as a crash. The same address handles it.

FAQ

Do you want the whole SDK folder zipped?

No. It is large, it is Google’s components plus your guest, and it can contain more than the bug. Send the facts above and a picture. If I need a specific log, I will ask for that log.

Can I get a remote session?

No. Email is the channel. I do not have a remote-desktop product, and I do not want one pointed at your Mac.

I found a workaround. Should I still write?

Yes, if the workaround was not obvious. Say what you changed and what the screen did before and after. That is how a checklist gets a new line that is true.

Where is the checklist if I want to try before I write?

The common problems, in order. If you already walked it, say which step you were on when you wrote.
