What it costs in the US

BlueStacks says Prime costs US$7.99 a month in every region where it is sold, and the United States is one of them. The free version stays free, with ads.

MuMu Player Pro needs a membership on the Mac after the 7-day trial. The help page lists US$12 per 30 days, US$36 per 90 days and US$144 a year as standard prices, with promo prices of US$10, US$27 and US$72 when I checked. The final amount in your currency appears before you pay.

Genymotion lists its prices in US dollars only. UTM is free on GitHub, and the same app costs $9.99 on the US Mac App Store.

Each option in a few lines

BlueStacks Air

Built for Apple Silicon and free to use, with ads. Many games come with preset controls. The optional Prime membership removes the ads. BlueStacks has no official root option; community mods exist, but they are unofficial.

MuMu Player Pro

Runs on Macs with M-series chips and has keyboard, mouse and gamepad mapping. The Mac version stops working without an active membership once the 7-day trial ends.

Android Studio Emulator

Google’s official emulator, free and open source as part of AOSP. It is excellent for building apps, but it comes with the whole IDE and has no game key mapping. System images without Google Play allow adb root.

Genymotion Desktop

A tool for QA and app testing. It is free for personal use with limited features, and paid licences unlock the rest. On Apple Silicon it needs Rosetta and offers Android 11 and later. Root can be turned on per device.

UTM

A free, open-source virtual machine app. You can install an Android build in it, but it is not an Android emulator out of the box and has no game controls. The Mac App Store version is the same app; paying gets you automatic updates and supports the project.

Not available for Apple Silicon Macs

LDPlayer’s FAQ says it only has a Windows version, and MEmu’s system requirements list Windows only. NoxPlayer’s support page says it does not support the Apple M1 chip. Waydroid is free and open source, but it runs on Linux. If a site offers a “Mac version” of one of these, it is not an official build.

Which one should you pick?

  • Free games and quick APK installs on an M-series Mac: SiliconPlay or BlueStacks Air.
  • You already use MuMu and want its game presets: MuMu Player Pro.
  • You build Android apps: Android Studio Emulator.
  • You test apps with sensors and many device profiles: Genymotion Desktop.
  • You want a general virtual machine to experiment with: UTM.

How SiliconPlay works

Under the hood, SiliconPlay runs Google’s official Android Emulator, with Android 16 running natively on the Mac’s ARM cores. The Mac app around it, the interface and the control mapping, is my own work. Root is optional and off by default. The shortcuts to learn first:

  • Command-I: import an APK and install it into Android.
  • F12 or Command-K: switch between Typing and Game keys.
  • Command-Shift-G: show or hide the on-screen game buttons.

FAQ

Is there a free Android emulator for a Mac M1?

Yes. SiliconPlay, BlueStacks Air and the Android Studio Emulator are free on M1 through M5. BlueStacks Air shows ads unless you pay for Prime.

Does LDPlayer work on a Mac?

No. LDPlayer says it only has a Windows version. MEmu is Windows only too, and NoxPlayer does not support the Apple M1 chip.

Which emulator is best for games on an M-series Mac?

Look at SiliconPlay, BlueStacks Air and MuMu Player Pro. All three map the keyboard to touch controls. Try your own game, because results vary by game, Mac and settings.
