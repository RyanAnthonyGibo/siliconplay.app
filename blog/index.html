Notes on SiliconPlay, a free Android emulator for Apple Silicon Macs. Updated 12 November 2026.

The guides at the top are the ones I would hand to someone setting the app up today: real failure order, adb, Play versus a single APK, per-chip expectations without invented frame rates, and how to move off another emulator. SiliconPlay runs on Apple silicon from M1 through M6. It is not compatible with Intel Macs. I have tested it on macOS Tahoe and macOS 27 Golden Gate. English is the version I maintain.
