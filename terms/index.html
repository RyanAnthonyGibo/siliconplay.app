What SiliconPlay is

SiliconPlay is a free Mac app that runs Android on Apple silicon, from M1 through M6, including Pro, Max, and Ultra. It is not compatible with Intel Macs. I have tested it on macOS Tahoe and macOS 27 Golden Gate. Other macOS versions are welcome as reports, not as a certification.

The app is built on the official Android ARM64 emulator. I wrote the Mac app around it: the window, the on-screen game controls, optional root, and the shortcuts. The graphics translation libraries are rewritten for compatibility and frame rate. I did not write Android, and I do not speak for Google or Apple. The licenses page names the third-party components that ship with a build. The about page is the plain account of what version 1.0.0 does.

The DMG does not contain the emulator or the system image. The first launch downloads Google’s components into a folder you choose, after you accept the Android SDK license. That license is Google’s. These terms do not replace it.
