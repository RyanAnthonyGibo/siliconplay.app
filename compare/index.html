Who should pick what

  • SiliconPlay. You want a free emulator for games and quick APK checks on Apple Silicon.
  • BlueStacks Air. You want a gaming-focused app that is free with ads and can open more than one instance.
  • MuMu Player Pro. You want a gaming app and you are fine with paid after a 7-day trial.
  • Android Studio Emulator. You are developing and you want the official tools, including the IDE.
  • Genymotion Desktop. You are in QA, you need sensor simulation, and Rosetta is acceptable.
  • UTM. You want a free general-purpose VM and you are not playing 3D games.
  • AVDRun. You want native multi-instance and you are fine with paid after a trial.
  • Mac Android Subsystem. You want each app in its own window, you can do without Google Play, and a pre-release is OK.
  • Mac.apk. You want to try running apps without an emulator, on macOS 26.4 or later, and an alpha is OK.
