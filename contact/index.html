What to include if something failed

A useful report names the chip (M1–M6, and Pro, Max, or Ultra if the Mac has that), the macOS version, and the game or the APK. I have tested SiliconPlay on macOS Tahoe and macOS 27 Golden Gate. Other versions are welcome as reports, not as a promise. Say whether the failure was the first-run download, installing a package, drawing, or the keyboard. If the package was a single APK, a split set, or an XAPK, say which. If you had the Play tablet or the Google APIs tablet, say which.

Version 1.0.0 can save a screenshot and can record the screen. Those actions are named in the Shortcuts list. The key is drawn in the app’s menu, so I am not going to guess it here. A picture of the actual error beats a sentence that says “it crashed.”

I cannot see your Google Play password, and I do not want it. Do not send it. Do not send a license key unless I asked for that specific key in reply. The privacy notice explains what the app does send, which is not the contents of your email until you choose to write.

What this address is not

I am not Apple and I am not Google. Account problems inside a game, a purchase inside Play, and a ban under a game’s own terms are the publisher’s. SiliconPlay does not modify games. Optional root is off by default and does not change that.

The privacy notice is how you use rights under the LGPD and the GDPR, including access, correction, and deletion. Email siliconplay.app@gmail.com for those requests too. Say that the message is a privacy request so I do not file it as a bug.
